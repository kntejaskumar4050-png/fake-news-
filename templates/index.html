Pipeline

From raw text to a reasoned verdict

Every analysis runs through the same reproducible pipeline used during training — no shortcuts, no random guesses.

01

Ingest & clean

The article is stripped of HTML, URLs and special characters, lowercased, tokenised and filtered for stopwords before anything touches the model.

  • HTML & entity removal
  • Lowercase + punctuation cleanup
  • NLTK stopword filtering
02

Neural analysis

Tokens are converted to integer IDs using a vocabulary fitted only on training data, padded to 256 steps and passed through an embedding + LSTM network.

  • 20,000-word vocabulary
  • 128-dimensional embeddings
  • LSTM & BiLSTM classifiers
03

Measure & explain

The sigmoid output becomes a class and a confidence score. You also see both models' outputs, token coverage and a plain-language explanation.

  • Confidence + decision margin
  • Per-model comparison
  • Stored history & analytics
Two architectures

LSTM vs BiLSTM

Both models share the same embedding layer and training split. The only architectural difference is the direction in which the sequence is read.

Honest by design

Confidence is presented as model certainty, never as factual truth. Short inputs and low token coverage trigger an explicit reliability warning.

What this system does — and does not do

The classifier learns statistical patterns that distinguish articles in its training corpus. It can be fooled, and it cannot verify claims, sources or events. A “REAL” verdict is not a guarantee of accuracy; always consult primary sources.
